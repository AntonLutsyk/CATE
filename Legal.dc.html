The small print, kept short.

What we collect, what you agree to, and how we keep CATÉ usable for everyone. Questions go to hello@cate.org.ua.

Privacy Terms Accessibility

Privacy

We collect only what a shelter needs to consider your application: your name, contact details, home situation and the answers you give. Saved cats and drafts stay in your browser until you apply.

Your application is shared with the one shelter you apply to — nobody else. We don't sell data or run advertising trackers. You can download or delete everything from your account at any time.

Terms

CATÉ connects you with partner shelters. The shelter makes every adoption decision and sets its own fee; fees go to the shelter in full.

Please apply honestly and for one cat at a time unless a shelter suggests a bonded pair. Profiles are written by shelter staff; if something looks wrong, tell us and we'll check with them.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA: every page works with a keyboard, has visible focus, readable contrast and descriptive photo text, and respects reduced-motion settings.

If anything gets in your way, email hello@cate.org.ua and we'll reply within two working days — and help you apply by phone in the meantime.
